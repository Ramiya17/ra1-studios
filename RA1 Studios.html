Questions

Good to know

What do you need from me to start?

A short brief is enough: what the video is for, who will watch it, the mood you want and any brand assets such as a logo, colors or reference clips. We shape the rest with you.

How long does a video take?

Short reels and social ads usually take a few days. Longer brand films and music visuals take longer. We confirm the timeline once we have read your brief.

What formats and quality do I get?

Videos are delivered in up to 4K, in vertical 9:16, square 1:1 and widescreen 16:9, ready to upload.

Can you use my logo, product and brand colors?

Yes. Send your assets and we build the look of the video around your brand, including titles, end cards and color grade.

Will the video be labeled as AI-generated?

Platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube ask creators to disclose realistic AI video. We follow each platform's rules and can add the right labels for you.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. We share drafts along the way and apply your feedback before the final delivery. We agree the number of revision rounds in your quote.
